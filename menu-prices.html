Joy's Food — menu & prices

Fill in a price for every dish. Correct any spelling, tick the veg box where it belongs, and add or delete rows as needed. Then press Save and send the file back.

Notice (hours) is how much warning you need before a slot to cook it. Meals and fries are set to 12, powders to 0 because they are made ahead and stored. Veg ticked means the dish carries the green marker on the menu.
What "Save" does It writes your entries back into this same file. Chrome will ask you where to save — pick this same menu-prices.html and choose Replace. If your browser can't do that, it downloads a fresh copy instead, which works just as well. Your typing is also kept in the browser automatically, so a stray refresh won't lose it.
